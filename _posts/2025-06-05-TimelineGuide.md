# Your Guide to the SGX3 HackHPC@ADMI25 Hackathon Timeline

Welcome to the **SGX3 HackHPC@ADMI25 Hackathon** — a hands-on, fast-paced event where you'll explore *reproducible science in high-performance computing (HPC)* using real research papers and powerful tools.

This guide is for future undergraduate **participants and Science Gateway developers** who want to understand the flow of the event, how to prepare, and what deliverables you'll need by the end.

📅 **Event Dates**: June 23–27, 2025  
📍 **Location**: Online (via Zoom)  
🎯 **Goal**: Evaluate and document the reproducibility of papers from **ICSE 2023** and **Supercomputing 2024 (SC24)** using open science practices.

---

## 🗓️ Daily Schedule at a Glance

Each day is structured into **two sessions** — morning and afternoon — to help teams collaborate, share progress, and receive feedback. Here’s what to expect:

---

### **Monday, June 23: Kick-Off and Team Formation**

- **10:00 AM EDT – Kick-Off**
  - Learn about the hackathon goals, timeline, and how to choose your paper.
  - Review code repositories and reproducibility artifacts from ICSE and SC24.
  - Form your teams!

- **3:00 PM EDT – Team Introductions**
  - Create a fun **1-slide, 1-minute intro**:
    - Team name
    - Members
    - Theme song 🎵
    - Custom Zoom background 🎨

---

### **Tuesday, June 24: Planning and First Check-In**

- **10:00 AM EDT – Project Planning**
  - Each team presents a **2-slide, 2-minute** project plan:
    - Key tasks (at least 5)
    - Assigned roles for each member

- **3:00 PM EDT – Progress Check-In #1**
  - Update slides should include:
    - Technology used (e.g., Docker, HPC cluster)
    - Bottlenecks or questions

---

### **Wednesday, June 25: Midweek Check-Ins**

- **10:00 AM & 3:00 PM EDT – Check-Ins**
  - Continue refining your work and sharing updates.
  - Keep your slides focused on:
    - Current priorities
    - Updated task list
    - Challenges or roadblocks

---

### **Thursday, June 26: Progress and Prep**

- **10:00 AM & 3:00 PM EDT – Check-Ins**
  - Use these sessions to:
    - Finalize testing
    - Polish documentation and start final deliverables
    - Make sure you’re ready for Friday

---

### **Friday, June 27: Final Stretch and Presentations**

- **10:00 AM EDT – Final Check-In**
  - Last update before the big showcase. Check all boxes.

- **1:00 PM EDT – Final Presentations**
  - Present your project to judges and fellow teams!
  - Deliverables due (see below ⬇️)

---

## ✅ Final Deliverables

To wrap up your hackathon project, your team will submit:

### 📂 GitHub Repository
- `README.md`: Project overview and how it supports reproducible science
- Source code and scripts (well-commented)
- Final project **poster (PDF)**
- Final presentation slides (PDF):
  - Team info
  - Tech used (HPC resources, GPUs, tools)
  - Paper scorecards and evaluation criteria

### 🌐 Website or Portal
- Display of **scorecard results**
- Explanation of your reproducibility metrics
- Team member profiles (photos + GitHub/LinkedIn links)

### 🏆 Bonus: Gateways 2025 Poster Submission
- Show proof of your poster submission (screenshot of confirmation)
- Earn **+5 bonus points**!

---

## 💡 Tips for Future Teams

- **Be ready to present!** Every session includes short, timed updates.
- **Communicate daily** via Zoom, Slack, or your favorite collab tools.
- **Document as you go** — don’t wait until the last day.
- **Use version control** and share your progress early.
- **Ask for help!** Mentors and organizers are here to support you.
